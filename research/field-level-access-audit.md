# Personnel-Level Phenomenon, Unit, Role Heterogeneity, and Access Audit — Issue #9

Status: **PARTIAL — FIELD ACCESS / INTERNAL PERSONNEL EVIDENCE NOT YET VERIFIED.**

This audit uses only the official Renstra (`SRC-007`) and currently registered institutional/scholarly evidence. It does not assume participant access, internal appraisal access, or organization-wide digital-task homogeneity.

## 1. What the current evidence establishes

### Organizational heterogeneity is material

The Renstra describes a substantially differentiated Korbrimob structure. It records Pasukan Pelopor; Pasukan Gegana with specialized Wanteror, Penjinakan Bom/Jibom, KBRN, and Bantuan Teknis units; Satuan Intel Brimob; Satuan Latihan; Pasukan Brimob regional formations; and a Technology Information and Communication field in the organizational structure. The Renstra also lists specialized competence/equipment requirements, including Jibom technician levels, Wanteror equipment, cyber/intelligence strengthening, unmanned-aircraft capability, tactical surveillance, and technology/data-analytics-based operations.

**Implication:** the proposed individual-level population cannot be treated as occupationally homogeneous. Technology use, competence-development exposure, operational demands, performance criteria, and leadership arrangements are likely to vary by satker/function/rank.

### Personnel-level study remains plausible but not yet proven feasible

The working dissertation architecture is HRM at the individual personnel level. The Renstra provides organization/program-level problems and strategies. A person-level study is therefore justified only if the project can observe meaningful variation in:

- personnel experience of leadership;
- personnel exposure/perception of competence-development practices;
- employee attitude/capability;
- person-level work performance.

Current repository evidence does not yet prove access to those data.

## 3. Leadership level and nesting

### Current evidence

The Renstra identifies leadership weakness in development, operations, control, and supervision, but the level is not specified as an employee-perception construct. Korbrimob personnel are organized under commanders/supervisors and units.

### Recommended employee-level referent if leadership survives

If a perception-based leadership construct is retained, the most defensible referent is:

**the respondent’s immediate operational/direct supervisor or commander whose behavior the respondent can observe consistently.**

Avoid asking respondents to evaluate “Korbrimob leadership” or the top commander abstractly.

### Nesting risk

Multiple respondents may rate the same commander and share unit conditions. Therefore independence cannot be assumed. Required later checks include commander/unit identifiers that preserve confidentiality, cluster sizes, and ICC/design-effect assessment before choosing a single-level or multilevel/cluster-robust analysis.

## 4. Competence-development exposure

The Renstra confirms multiple forms of development: Latharkatpuan, certification, curriculum adaptation, instructor development, personalized learning, role rotation, and other capability-building strategies.

What is unknown is whether personnel receive these practices with comparable frequency, quality, relevance, or perceived support. For an individual-level X2, field data should establish:

- which development opportunities respondents actually receive;
- perceived quality/relevance rather than only attendance;
- unit/rank/role differences in access;
- whether rotation, individualized support, formal training, and certification are common enough to form a coherent perceived practice construct.

## 5. Digital-task heterogeneity

### Evidence supporting heterogeneity

The Renstra identifies specialized units and capabilities, including Wanteror, Jibom, KBRN, technical support, intelligence/cyber-related strengthening, unmanned-aircraft capability, and technology/data-analytics operations. It also lists technology-specific equipment such as tactical surveillance systems, communication systems, robot surveillance, thermal/optical systems, intelligence monitoring/analytics, and drones.

### Consequence for Digital Competence

A single organization-wide digital competence score is defensible only if there is a common core of digital tasks across the final respondent population. Otherwise, observed score differences may reflect **job exposure/opportunity** rather than individual capability.

Before retaining Digital Competence as a moderator, field evidence should identify:

1. common digital tasks across target roles;
2. frequency/criticality of those tasks;
3. systems/equipment access;
4. task-specific technical training;
5. whether low digital use means low competence or simply low job requirement.

A digital-task-intensity measure or bounded high-digital population may be necessary.

## 6. Individual work performance: feasible sources

Current Renstra indicators are not an employee-level work-performance measure. If Y remains Individual Work Performance, possible sources are:

| Source | Strength | Major risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Supervisor rating** | Reduces same-source bias; aligns performance with observable work | Command hierarchy, rater leniency/severity, access, confidentiality |
| **Administrative/formal appraisal** | Existing organizational record; lower respondent burden | Unknown construct validity; may be restricted/classified; may measure compliance/rank-specific criteria rather than IWP |
| **Self-report IWP** | Feasible and standardized; Indonesian IWPQ evidence exists | Common-method bias, social desirability, self-enhancement in hierarchical setting |
| **Multi-source combination** | Stronger triangulation | Highest operational/access complexity |

No source is currently verified as accessible.

## 7. Social-desirability and command-chain risk

Korbrimob is a hierarchical security organization. Personnel may perceive questions about commanders, commitment, competence, or performance as evaluative. This creates risks of:

- inflated leadership ratings;
- inflated commitment/performance ratings;
- fear of identification;
- unit-specific response pressure;
- nonresponse among critical respondents.

Any future field design should require clear research independence, anonymity/confidentiality, non-retaliation assurance, no direct supervisor access to individual responses, and careful collection logistics. These are design requirements, not yet verified permissions.

## 9. Preliminary field-level recommendations

### Leadership
Proceed only with a clear immediate-supervisor/commander referent and a sampling structure that can handle respondents nested under common leaders.

### Competence Development Practices
Strongly feasible in principle at the person level if the instrument measures **experienced/perceived organizational development practices**, not only whether programs exist.

### Affective Organizational Commitment
Measurable at the individual level, but relevance cannot be inferred from Renstra. Its retention depends on theory/literature and whether the field can guarantee sufficiently confidential attitude measurement.

### Digital Competence
Do not use as a universal moderator until the final population shows sufficiently common digital-task requirements or the design explicitly measures task intensity.

### Individual Work Performance
Keep as candidate individual outcome, but prioritize a source independent of predictors if access permits. If only self-report is feasible, causal language and common-method limitations must be sharply bounded.

## 10. Effect on the background manuscript

The current background remains defensible if it says:

- Korbrimob is organizationally and occupationally heterogeneous;
- technology-related capability is strategically important but not necessarily uniform across all roles;
- the study seeks a personnel-level explanation but existing Renstra evidence is organization/program level;
- final population, role boundary, and measurement source are still to be established.

The background should **not** state that the five candidate constructs are equally applicable to every Korbrimob person.
