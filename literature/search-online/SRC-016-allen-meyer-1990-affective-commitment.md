## Evidence retained
The article distinguishes affective commitment from continuance and normative commitment. Affective commitment refers to employees' emotional attachment to, identification with, and involvement in the organization. The study developed scales for the three components and reported empirical distinctions among them, while noting some relationship between affective and normative commitment.

## Research use
Provides the authoritative construct lineage for `Affective Organizational Commitment` and establishes why loyalty, obligation, retention cost, and generic organizational commitment should not be treated as interchangeable with AOC.

## Limits
- This source validates the construct lineage, not AOC's necessity as the Sudianto mediator.
- Exact choice among original/revised AOC scale versions requires a separate measurement decision.
- In a hierarchical security organization, socially desirable responses and overlap with loyalty/esprit-de-corps language require careful adaptation and discriminant testing.
