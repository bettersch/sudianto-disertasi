## Epistemic role in this dissertation

This Renstra is a **foundational primary institutional guideline** for:

1. defining the Korbrimob 2025–2029 strategic context and time boundary;
2. identifying officially documented organizational weaknesses and problems relevant to HRM;
3. testing whether proposed dissertation constructs have a defensible locus connection;
4. checking whether practical implications remain aligned with Korbrimob priorities and targets.

It is **not** treated as:

- peer-reviewed evidence for causal relationships;
- proof that all personnel experience the documented weaknesses equally;
- employee-level measurement of leadership, commitment, digital competence, development practices, or individual work performance;
- evidence of a scholarly research gap by itself.
