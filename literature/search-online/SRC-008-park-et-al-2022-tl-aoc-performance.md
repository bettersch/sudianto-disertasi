## Evidence retained
The study used data from 600 full-time employees in Korean private organizations and SEM/bootstrap. Transformational leadership, affective organizational commitment, job performance, and employee engagement were examined together. Employee engagement partially mediated the relationship between transformational leadership and affective organizational commitment and fully mediated the relationship between transformational leadership and job performance.

## Research use
Useful as the closest HRD-oriented anchor showing that leadership, employee attitude, and performance can be linked through an intervening employee state. It **does not** show that affective organizational commitment mediates transformational leadership and performance. It also does not establish construct fit for Korbrimob.

## Limits
Different national/organizational context; observational survey; mediator is employee engagement. Use to motivate mechanism uncertainty, not causal proof.
