# Source Note

- SRC ID: SRC-014
- Related Issue: #15
- Type: peer-reviewed journal article
- Citation: Indrayanto, A., Burgess, J., Dayaram, K., & Noermijati. (2014). A case study of transformational leadership and para-police performance in Indonesia. *Policing: An International Journal, 37*(2), 373–388.
- DOI: 10.1108/PIJPSM-03-2013-0029
- Publisher: Emerald Publishing
- Access: publisher abstract/full metadata verified; accessed 2026-10-11.

## Evidence retained
The study surveyed/interviewed leaders and employees in an Indonesian Civilian Para-Police Force (n=132) and used multiple regression. Transformational leadership did not directly influence employee performance; trust and commitment were reported as mediating variables.

## Research use
Provides older but contextually useful Indonesian para-police evidence that the leadership-performance relationship may depend on employee relational/attitudinal mechanisms rather than a simple direct association.

## Limits
The study is more than a decade old, institution-specific, cross-sectional, and uses a small sample. Its organizational commitment construct is not automatically equivalent to affective organizational commitment. It cannot establish the present Korbrimob mechanism or validate transformational leadership as the final leadership construct.
