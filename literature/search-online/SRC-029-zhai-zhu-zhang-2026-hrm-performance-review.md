## Evidence retained
The review maps and synthesizes 3,503 peer-reviewed articles published across 156 leading journals from 1995 to 2025. It identifies the HRM–performance 'black box', multilevel effects, configurational HRM architectures, and contextual contingencies as central themes. The review emphasizes that HRM effects on performance are transmitted through multiple capability, attitude, behavior, and well-being mechanisms rather than a single automatic direct pathway.

## Research use
This review supports framing Sudianto's study within the HRM–performance black-box problem. It also cautions against treating one HR practice, one employee attitude, or one moderator as a universally sufficient explanation. It strengthens the requirement to align mechanism, level of analysis, and boundary conditions.

## Limits
The review concerns HRM systems broadly, not Korbrimob or competence-development practices specifically. It supports a research framing and theory-selection discipline, not any specific structural path.
