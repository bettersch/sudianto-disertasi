## Evidence retained
The article developed the Global Transformational Leadership (GTL) scale using 1,440 subordinates who assessed 695 branch managers in an Australian financial organization. Exploratory and confirmatory factor analyses supported a single transformational-leadership construct, with satisfactory reliability plus convergent and discriminant validity evidence.

## Research use
GTL is a plausible concise candidate if `Transformational Leadership` survives the construct lock, especially because a subordinate can rate a directly observed leader. Its brevity is attractive in a long dissertation questionnaire.

## Limits
- The Renstra establishes a leadership problem, not transformational leadership specifically.
- Australian financial-sector validation does not establish content validity for Korbrimob command/operational leadership.
- Exact item wording, translation/adaptation procedure, and permission/licensing must be verified before instrument construction.
- GTL should be compared with rival leadership constructs before selection.
