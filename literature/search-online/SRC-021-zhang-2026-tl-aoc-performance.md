## Evidence retained
The study used a cross-sectional survey of 268 full-time employees in manufacturing, service, and technology organizations in mainland China. Transformational leadership, affective organizational commitment (AOC), and employee performance were measured with multi-item scales and analysed with SEM and bootstrap confidence intervals. Transformational leadership was positively associated with AOC and employee performance; AOC was positively associated with employee performance; and the indirect association through AOC was statistically significant while the direct association remained significant, consistent with partial mediation.

## Research use
This is direct recent evidence that AOC can operate as an intervening employee-attitude mechanism between transformational leadership and employee performance. It materially strengthens the empirical plausibility of the proposed leadership–AOC–performance mechanism.

## Limits
The design is cross-sectional and observational, so temporal/causal ordering is not established. The sample is not a police/security organization and performance is survey-measured. The study itself also recognizes that commitment is not the only pathway. It cannot establish that AOC is the *best* mediator for Korbrimob or that the same effect generalizes to a hierarchical high-risk public-security context.
