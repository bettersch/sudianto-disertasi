## Evidence retained
A sample of 476 Turkish healthcare professionals was analyzed using structural equation modeling. The study examined Transformational Leadership, employees’ Affective Commitment, and Individual Performance. The abstract reports that Affective Commitment mediated the relationship between Transformational Leadership and Individual Performance.

## Research use
This is a direct structural predecessor showing that the Transformational Leadership–Affective Commitment–Individual Performance mechanism predates the Sudianto dissertation. It is therefore evidence against claiming novelty from that three-construct combination. Its value is to establish precedent and to sharpen the remaining mechanism/context/boundary puzzle.

## Limits
The study is observational, sector-specific, and outside a police/public-security context. Abstract-level evidence does not establish effect-size robustness, temporal ordering, or generalizability to Korbrimob. It does not show that AOC is the unique mediator or that Digital Competence moderates the AOC–performance relationship.
