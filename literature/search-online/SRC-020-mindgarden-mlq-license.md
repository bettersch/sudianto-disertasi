# Source Note

- SRC ID: SRC-020
- Related Issue: #10
- Type: official instrument publisher/licensing source (non-scholarly)
- Instrument: Multifactor Leadership Questionnaire (MLQ / MLQ 5X Short)
- Publisher/licensor: Mind Garden, Inc.
- Accessed: 2026-10-11
- URL role: official licensing/product page; not theoretical or psychometric evidence.

## Evidence retained
Mind Garden states that research administration of the MLQ requires a purchased administration license when the instrument is reproduced/administered outside its hosted system. The standard MLQ 5X Short has leader and rater forms and 45 questions; licensing conditions restrict reproduction and distribution of copyrighted items.

## Research use
Measurement-feasibility evidence only. It shows that selecting MLQ has cost, permissions, reproduction, and translation implications that must be resolved before the questionnaire is finalized.

## Limits
This source does not determine whether MLQ is theoretically superior to GTL or other leadership measures. Instrument choice must follow construct fit, respondent burden, psychometrics, context, and permission feasibility.
