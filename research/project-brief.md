## Stage 1 evidence boundary

Registered Stage 1 evidence establishes organizational activities, priorities, and stated capability targets only. It does not establish:

- individual personnel perceptions of leadership;
- individual exposure to competence-development practices;
- affective organizational commitment levels;
- organization-wide digital competence levels;
- individual work-performance levels;
- causal effects of training, leadership, or technology capability;
- or a scholarly research gap.

The digital/technology evidence is demonstrably role-specific in at least one retained source, so organization-wide relevance of digital competence must be tested rather than assumed.

## Evidence requirements before strengthening the framing

- Important locus claims require a registered institutional/public source with an actual locator and access status.
- Important scholarly claims require verified source metadata and source notes; abstracts support only what they actually report.
- A gap is not established by a new location, a new variable combination, or the phrase “few studies.”
- Theory is not selected because it appears compatible with the working title; competing explanations must be inspected.
- Method is not selected because it was used in prior studies or requested in prior conversation; it follows the research problem and RQs.
- The digital-competence moderation proposition requires specific theoretical and contextual support and may be revised if the evidence does not sustain it.

## Scope boundary

The repository does **not yet** claim that:

- Korbrimob or its personnel have generally low performance;
- affective commitment is the correct mediator;
- digital competence is the correct moderator or that a particular moderation path is justified;
- Social Exchange Theory, AMO, or any other theory is the final theoretical backbone;
- PLS-SEM, SEM, mixed methods, cross-sectional survey, or any other method has been approved;
- the proposed model is novel;
- the literature review is complete or systematic;
- ethics approval, participant access, data access, or instrument permission exists.

Those decisions are made only after the evidence required by the research flow is available and reviewed.
