# Source Note

- SRC ID: SRC-013
- Related Issue: #15
- Type: peer-reviewed journal article
- Citation: Ochoa Pacheco, P., & Coello-Montecel, D. (2023). Does psychological empowerment mediate the relationship between digital competencies and job performance? *Computers in Human Behavior, 140*, 107575.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.chb.2022.107575
- Access: Open Access under a Creative Commons license; publisher full text verified; accessed 2026-10-11.

## Evidence retained
The study collected data from 357 professionals and used structural equation modeling. Digital competencies were positively associated with job performance and psychological empowerment, and psychological empowerment was positively associated with job performance. Psychological empowerment partially mediated the digital-competence/job-performance relationship.

## Research use
Supports digital competence as a potentially job-relevant individual capability and demonstrates that its relationship with performance may operate through another psychological mechanism.

## Limits
Digital competence was modeled as an antecedent, not a moderator. The study therefore does **not** validate the dissertation’s proposed moderation role. Occupational digital-task requirements may differ substantially from Korbrimob roles.
