# Source Note

- SRC ID: SRC-010
- Related Issue: #15
- Type: peer-reviewed journal article
- Citation: Moreira, A., Tomás, C., & Antunes, A. (2024). The Mediating Effect of Affective Commitment on the Relationship between Competence Development and Turnover Intentions: Does This Relationship Depend on the Employee’s Generation? *Administrative Sciences, 14*(5), 97.
- DOI: 10.3390/admsci14050097
- Access: Gold Open Access; publisher full text verified on MDPI; accessed 2026-10-11.

## Evidence retained
The sample comprised 2,123 employees in Portuguese organizations. Organizational competence-development practices were operationalized through training, individualized support, and functional rotation. These practices were positively associated with affective commitment. Affective commitment mediated relationships between competence-development practices and turnover intention, with patterns varying by generation.

## Research use
Provides a strong HRM/HRD construct anchor for competence-development practices and supports treating development as an organizational practice rather than as the employee’s existing competence level. It also provides evidence that employee attitudes can be associated with perceived organizational development practices.

## Limits
Outcome is turnover intention rather than work performance; cross-sectional/self-report limitations apply; generational findings are context-specific. It does not establish the proposed Sudianto performance mediation model.
