## 3. Main problem

### Evidenced locus problem

Korbrimob is actively developing personnel capability and modernizing operations while its official 2025–2029 Renstra simultaneously records specific weaknesses/problems in HR capability, technology mastery, instructor professionalism, leadership, professionalism/management, and operational effectiveness. This provides a stronger institutional basis for asking what personnel-level HRM mechanisms matter.

However, the Renstra does not tell us which academic constructs best represent those problems. In particular:

- an official statement of weak leadership does not establish that **Transformational Leadership** is the correct leadership construct;
- Latharkatpuan, training, certification, personalized learning, and rotation create a strong competence-development context but do not automatically define the final academic construct **Competence Development Practices**;
- **Affective Organizational Commitment** is not established by the Renstra as the necessary employee-state mechanism;
- technology and data-analytics priorities make **Digital Competence** relevant but do not establish universal task relevance or a moderation role;
- organizational/program performance indicators are not equivalent to **Individual Work Performance**.

The immediate dissertation problem is therefore a **construct–field translation problem**: determine which employee-level constructs validly explain the officially documented HRM/capability problem domains and can be measured in the accessible Korbrimob population without cross-level or conceptual mismatch.

This is still a problem-framing direction, not yet a scholarly research gap.
