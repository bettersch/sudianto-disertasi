# Closest-Study Audit Before Model Lock

Status: **EVIDENCE SYNTHESIS COMPLETE — MODEL/CONSTRUCT LOCK STILL CONDITIONAL**  
Related Issue: #21  
Primary locus guideline: SRC-007  
Theory audit input: `research/theory-rival-mechanism-audit.md`

## 1. Purpose

This audit identifies studies that are structurally closest to the Sudianto working model. The purpose is not to prove that no identical five-variable model exists. The purpose is to determine which relationships are already empirically familiar, which remain contested, which mechanisms compete with Affective Organizational Commitment (AOC), and whether Digital Competence has a defensible role as a boundary condition.

The working architecture under review contains Transformational Leadership, Competence Development Practices, AOC, Digital Competence, and Individual Work Performance in the Korbrimob Polri context.

## 3. What is already sufficiently established to reject as novelty

### 3.1 Transformational Leadership, commitment, and performance are not a novel combination

The relationship among Transformational Leadership, organizational attachment/commitment, and employee performance has already been tested in multiple sectors and countries. Zhang (2026) directly tests AOC as a partial mediator. Earlier evidence also supports commitment-related mediation, while Sudha et al. and Indrayanto et al. show that direct leadership–performance relationships can weaken or disappear in public/hierarchical contexts.

**Implication:** the dissertation must not claim novelty merely because it links Transformational Leadership, AOC, and performance.

### 3.2 Development/HR practices, AOC, and employee outcomes are not a novel mechanism class

Moreira et al. directly connect organizational competence-development practices with AOC. Wanto & Kusumatuti show an Indonesian-police analogue involving training/competence, AOC, and job performance. Hasinat et al. show a broader HRM–AOC–multidimensional-performance mechanism.

**Implication:** the dissertation cannot claim that using AOC to open the HRM–performance black box is itself novel. The value must come from a sharper construct, mechanism, context boundary, design, or unresolved empirical pattern.

### 3.3 Digital competence and employee performance are already connected in the literature

Ochoa Pacheco & Coello-Montecel support an association between digital competencies and job performance and show psychological empowerment as a mechanism. Therefore, a simple Digital Competence–performance association is not a sufficient contribution.

**Implication:** Digital Competence must have a theoretically precise role rather than being added because digitalization is contemporary.

## 4. What remains contested or insufficiently established

### 4.1 The intervening employee-state mechanism is not settled

AOC is empirically defensible, but the closest studies also support employee engagement, trust, positive affect, psychological need fulfilment, psychological empowerment, and organizational support-related interpretations. The literature therefore supports **mechanism plurality**, not a single settled black box.

AOC's strongest argument in Sudianto is that both leadership and competence-development practices can be interpreted as favorable organizational treatment/investment, which may strengthen emotional attachment and reciprocal behavior. Its weakest point is that Korbrimob's own Renstra does not diagnose an AOC deficit and even names esprit de corps, discipline, loyalty, and dedication as institutional strengths.

**Interim conclusion:** AOC should remain **CONDITIONAL RETAIN**, not be presented as an established Korbrimob problem or the uniquely correct mediator.

### 4.2 The role of Digital Competence as moderator remains the weakest structural proposition

The strongest verified moderation studies do not show Digital Competence moderating a generic AOC–performance relationship. They show digital literacy/competence altering employee responses to **explicitly digitalized work or digital-technology requirements**.

That distinction is theoretically important. A capability is most likely to condition performance when the task requires that capability. If a sampled job has little digital intensity, high or low digital competence has no clear reason to change how organizational attachment translates into work performance.

**Interim conclusion:** Digital Competence may remain in the dissertation, but the AOC–performance moderation should be retained only when digital-task exposure is demonstrated or bounded by the design.

### 4.3 Direct effects should not be removed merely to create mediation

The closest studies show partial mediation, full mediation, weak mediation, and non-significant direct effects depending on context. Competence-development practices may also influence performance through actual capability acquisition rather than only through attachment.

**Implication:** if AOC is retained as mediator, direct relationships from the antecedent constructs to IWP should remain empirically testable unless strong theory says otherwise.

## 5. Strongest context-relevant evidence

Two studies are especially important for the Korbrimob rationale:

1. **Indrayanto et al. (2014)** shows in an Indonesian para-police context that Transformational Leadership did not directly explain performance; trust and commitment transmitted the relationship. This makes mechanism questions substantively relevant in a quasi-security/hierarchical setting.
2. **Wanto & Kusumatuti (2023)** shows in Indonesian police personnel that training/competence-related factors were associated with AOC and performance, with AOC operating as a mediator; importantly, competence did not have a significant direct relationship with performance in that study.

These studies do not prove the Sudianto model. Instead, they demonstrate that in Indonesian police/para-police contexts, **how leadership and development inputs translate into personnel outcomes is already an empirical question with indirect and sometimes non-significant direct relationships**.

## 6. The surviving research puzzle

After removing artificial novelty claims, the defensible unresolved puzzle is narrower:

> Korbrimob Polri is simultaneously investing in leadership, competence development, certification, modernization, and technology/data-analytics capability while its 2025–2029 Renstra still records weaknesses in leadership, instructor professionalism, HR capability/technology mastery, professionalism/management, and operational effectiveness. Existing HRM and leadership studies indicate that such organizational inputs do not translate uniformly into individual performance and may operate through different employee-state mechanisms. It remains unresolved, for Korbrimob personnel, whether perceived leadership and competence-development practices are associated with individual work performance partly through affective organizational commitment, and whether any role for digital competence depends on the actual digital demands of the personnel roles studied.

This is a **mechanism-and-boundary-condition puzzle**, not a 'five variables have never been combined' gap.

## 8. Candidate architecture after the closest-study audit

### Preferred default: Candidate A — conservative HRM black-box model

Retain Transformational Leadership and Competence Development Practices as antecedent constructs, AOC as a candidate intervening employee attitude, and Individual Work Performance as the outcome. Estimate direct relationships as well as the indirect mechanism. Retain Digital Competence as a personnel capability/main effect or contextual boundary descriptor unless field evidence justifies an interaction.

**Why this is currently preferred:** every central component has a clear evidence chain and the model does not depend on an unsupported moderation proposition.

### Conditional alternative: Candidate B — bounded digital moderation

Retain the same core model and specify Digital Competence as a boundary condition only if the population is restricted to digitally intensive roles or digital-task requirements are explicitly measured and shown to be material across the sampled personnel.

**Why this remains conditional:** the moderation is theoretically plausible through ability–task complementarity, but direct precedent for AOC-by-Digital-Competence on performance remains unverified.

### Not preferred without major redesign: Candidate C — digital-demand model

Re-specify the model around explicit workplace digitalization/digital technology requirements and Digital Competence as a personal resource shaping appraisal/performance.

**Why not default:** it fits the recent digital literature better but materially changes the dissertation's HRM problem and would require at least one additional digital-demand construct.

## 9. Research-gap statement permitted after this audit

A defensible literature-gap statement can focus on three unresolved issues:

1. **Mechanism uncertainty:** leadership and development practices can relate to performance through multiple employee-state mechanisms; AOC is plausible but not uniquely established, particularly in hierarchical public-security organizations.
2. **Level/context translation:** Korbrimob's strategic HR problems and performance targets are organizational, while the dissertation concerns individual personnel outcomes; the employee-level translation is not supplied by the Renstra.
3. **Boundary-condition uncertainty:** digital competence is relevant to modernized operational work, but its performance value is likely contingent on task-specific digital demands, which are heterogeneous across Korbrimob roles.

The gap must not be framed as:

- 'no study combines all five variables';
- 'few studies exist in Indonesia';
- 'Brimob has never been studied';
- 'prior findings are inconsistent' without specifying the substantive reason for the inconsistency.

## 10. Implication for the background manuscript

The final background should now end with a sequence of claims that is narrower and better supported:

1. Korbrimob faces a documented HRM capability/leadership/technology modernization problem, not a proven generalized performance failure.
2. Leadership and competence-development inputs do not automatically translate into individual performance.
3. AOC is a theoretically and empirically defensible employee-attitude mechanism, but alternative mechanisms remain credible.
4. Digital Competence is a meaningful capability in the modernization agenda, but its moderating role cannot be generalized across heterogeneous personnel roles without evidence of digital-task requirements.
5. The dissertation therefore investigates an employee-level HRM-performance translation problem and tests, rather than assumes, the proposed mechanism and boundary condition.

## 11. Remaining conditions before final lock

The closest-study literature gate is now substantively satisfied. Issue #12 should **not** be passed until the outstanding field-dependent questions from Issue #9 are answered:

- final sampled population/unit scope;
- anonymity/command-chain separation during data collection;
- availability of supervisor-rated or administrative performance data;
- ability to code unit/function and leader anonymously;
- access to internal evaluation/training/HR evidence beyond the Renstra.

These items directly determine whether Digital Competence can be bounded, whether nesting must be addressed, and whether Individual Work Performance can be measured credibly.

No final causal claim, hypothesis, estimator, novelty claim, or digital moderation is authorized by this audit.
