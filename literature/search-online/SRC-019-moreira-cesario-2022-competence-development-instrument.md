## Evidence retained
The validation work treats organizational competence-development practices as a multidimensional employee-perception construct, with the recurring dimensions of training, individualized support, and functional rotation. The later large-sample study in SRC-010 applies the same three-domain structure.

## Research use
Useful for defining X2 as **organization-provided development practices experienced/perceived by employees**, rather than actual competence level or simple training attendance.

## Limits
- Academia Letters is not a strong final evidentiary anchor by itself; use together with SRC-010 and the original scale lineage.
- The three-domain instrument may not cover all Korbrimob-relevant development practices (e.g., certification, task-specific capability maintenance) and must not be expanded ad hoc without content validation.
- Item wording/translation and reuse permissions require verification.
