- Related Issue: #15
- Type: peer-reviewed journal article / measurement study
- Citation: Widyastuti, T., Ramdani, Z., Alim, S., & Harlianty, R. A. (2024). The Indonesian version of Individual Work Performance Questionnaire (IWPQ): Structure factor and criterion validity. *Psychological Research and Intervention, 7*(1), 1–16.
- DOI: 10.21831/pri.v7i1.76457
- Access: Open Access, CC BY 4.0; publisher article/PDF verified; accessed 2026-10-11.

## Evidence retained
Two independent Indonesian employee samples were used (Study 1 = 231; Study 2 = 378; total N = 609). CFA supported a three-factor IWPQ structure, and scores correlated positively with work engagement as criterion evidence.

## Research use
Supports the conceptual and measurement distinction between individual work performance and organizational/program performance in the Indonesian context. It provides a later measurement candidate if individual work performance survives the construct lock.

## Limits
The samples were not Korbrimob personnel. The study does not establish any performance deficit in Korbrimob and does not by itself establish content validity for high-risk public-security work.
