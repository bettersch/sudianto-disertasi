## Search interpretation and stopping note

The Stage 1 purpose is not an exhaustive news census. Searching stopped when the retained set covered: (a) Korbrimob's formal training/personnel-management function, (b) a current leadership-development activity, (c) a current broad competence-development statement tied to technology, (d) a concrete technology-capability activity, (e) a current strategic forum explicitly calling for SDM evaluation and operational modernization, and (f) limiting evidence that official leadership also described strong recent performance/professionalism. This set is sufficient to establish a bounded locus phenomenon while preventing a false inference that development activity proves organizational deficiency.

A direct public copy of the top-level Korbrimob Renstra 2025–2029 (`Kep/299/V/2025`) was not located through the structured searches. A subordinate-unit planning document and general Polri public-information pages referenced the strategic-planning framework, but they are not used here as a substitute for the top-level document.
