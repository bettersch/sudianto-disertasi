# Source Note

- SRC ID: SRC-009
- Related Issue: #15
- Type: peer-reviewed journal article
- Citation: Jiatong, W., Wang, Z., Alam, M., Murad, M., Gul, F., & Gill, S. A. S. (2022). The Impact of Transformational Leadership on Affective Organizational Commitment and Job Performance: The Mediating Role of Employee Engagement. *Frontiers in Psychology, 13*, 831060.
- DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2022.831060
- Access: Gold Open Access, full text verified on Frontiers; accessed 2026-10-11.

## Evidence retained
Data were collected from 845 hotel employees in China and analyzed with structural equation modeling. The article reports positive associations of transformational leadership with affective organizational commitment and job performance, while employee engagement partially mediated the examined relationships.

## Research use
Supports the relevance of leadership, affective commitment, and performance as related employee-level constructs, but also shows that employee engagement is a plausible alternative/intervening mechanism.

## Limits
Hospitality context, not public security; observational design; does not establish that transformational leadership is the correct leadership construct for Korbrimob or that AOC is the unique mediator.
