## Evidence retained
Across two independent longitudinal designs using daily and weekly repeated measurements, experienced episodes of transformational leadership were associated with basic psychological need fulfilment, increases in positive affect, and subsequent increases in work engagement. The studies support an affective/motivational pathway rather than an organizational-attachment pathway.

## Research use
This is high-value rival-mechanism evidence for Issue #11. It demonstrates that transformational leadership can operate through need fulfilment, affect regulation, and engagement. Therefore, AOC should not be treated as the uniquely implied mediator merely because leadership is transformational.

## Limits
The outcome studied was work engagement rather than individual work performance, and samples were not public-security personnel. The evidence challenges mediator uniqueness; it does not by itself prove that engagement should replace AOC in Korbrimob.
