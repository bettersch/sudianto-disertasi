## Evidence retained
The IWPQ was developed as a generic, short employee-level work-performance instrument. Development work identified a three-dimensional framework consisting of task performance, contextual performance, and counterproductive work behavior, with generic scales constructed using factor/Rasch analyses across occupational groups.

## Research use
Provides the construct lineage for `Individual Work Performance` and supports keeping employee work performance conceptually separate from organizational, unit, program, or strategic performance indicators.

## Limits
- A generic IWPQ does not automatically capture all performance requirements in high-risk, command-based Brimob work.
- Adaptive performance may be substantively relevant but should not be appended without construct justification and validation.
- Performance-source choice (self, supervisor, administrative, multi-source) remains an Issue #9 access/design question.
