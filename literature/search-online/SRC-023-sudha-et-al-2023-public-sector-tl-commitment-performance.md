## Evidence retained
The study used a quantitative cross-sectional design with 370 employees from Maldives civil-service offices and SEM/bootstrapping. Transformational leadership did not have a statistically significant direct relationship with job performance. Organisational commitment mediated the relationship, but the reported mediation effect size was small.

## Research use
This is valuable public-sector counterevidence against a simple assumption that transformational leadership necessarily has a strong direct relationship with employee performance. It supports keeping an intervening employee state in the model while requiring attention to effect size and public-sector boundary conditions.

## Limits
The commitment construct is broader than affective organizational commitment, the context is civil service rather than policing/high-risk security, and the design is cross-sectional. The paper does not validate the specific Korbrimob construct configuration.
